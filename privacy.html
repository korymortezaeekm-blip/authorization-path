Authorization Path – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Midwest Solutions LLC

This policy covers the Authorization Path app for Android (package com.authorizationpath.app), published by Midwest Solutions LLC ("we", "us").

Summary: the app does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for your email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It requests no Android permissions and has no analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

Information stored on your device

So that it can show your progress, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device only:

  • your answers, quiz and mock exam results, review schedule, mission progress, badges and the days you studied
  • your settings, such as theme and text size
  • the nickname you type for the greeting, if you type one
  • that you have read the disclaimer

Deleting it

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time with "Reset local data" in the app's settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved progress in your personal Google backup so that it can be restored on a new device. That backup is run by Google under your Google account and is not accessible to us.

Exports

If you use "Export progress", the app puts a copy of your progress on your clipboard as text. Where you paste and keep that text is up to you.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software and shares no data with anyone. It is distributed through Google Play. Google handles your purchase, download and account information under Google's own privacy policy, and we do not receive your payment details. If your version of the app offers an in-app unlock, the app asks Google Play only whether the unlock is owned.

Links to official web pages open in your web browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Exam registration

The app is not where you register for an exam, and it never asks for candidate or identity details. Registration happens only with the certification body and its testing provider.

Children

The app is a study tool intended for adult professionals and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and its effective date changed before that version is released. The current version is shown in the app and at https://korymortezaeekm-blip.github.io/authorization-path/privacy.html
