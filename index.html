Authorization Path

Independent practice for CGRC® candidates

Practice the judgment, not the trivia.

A calm, offline study app for Android phones and tablets. Original questions with a reason for every option, and one fictional system to take through the whole Risk Management Framework.

For Android · one purchase · no ads · no subscription · no account

Authorization Path is built for people preparing for the Certified in Governance, Risk and Compliance (CGRC®) exam, formerly CAP. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ISC2. Everything in it was written for this app, and your progress never leaves your device.

What's inside

  • 500 original questionsAcross all seven exam domains. Every option comes with its reason, right or wrong, and a source line where the point rests on a public publication.
  • More than multiple choiceExhibit, ordering, matching and multi-select items, so you practise reading a document and deciding, not just recalling a term.
  • The RMF MissionTake one fictional agency system from Prepare to Monitor in seven chapters, with scored decisions and artifacts you complete yourself.
  • Two mock examsTwo separate 125-item, 3-hour forms, with a practice mode and a stricter exam-day mode. Pause and pick up where you left off.
  • 280 flashcardsWith one spaced-review queue that mixes cards and the questions you missed.
  • Guides and a Frameworks deskRMF, CSF 2.0, FedRAMP, ISO/IEC 27001, CMMC, PCI DSS, HIPAA and GDPR, at the level a candidate needs.
  • An honest readiness estimateBy domain, with an explanation of how it is worked out. It says plainly that it is not an official score.
  • Comfortable to useDark and light themes, larger text sizes, reduced motion and screen-reader labels.
